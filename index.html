Voice and video, one-to-one or the whole group — with screen sharing when words aren't enough.

End-to-end encrypted

Your messages and media are sealed on your device. Only the people you send them to can open them.

Groups for your people

Android

Beta open

Google's closed testing takes three quick steps. Use the same Google account your phone's Play Store uses — otherwise Play won't recognise you as a tester.

  1. 1Join the testers group — one tap, this tells Google you're allowed in.
    groups.google.com/g/chatt-beta →
  2. 2Accept the test on the Google Play testing page.
    play.google.com/apps/testing →
  3. 3Install ChatT from the Play Store link that page shows you.
Join the Android beta

The Play Store listing only appears once you've done steps 1 and 2 — it's invisible to everyone else.

Already running ChatT from a hand-installed APK? Write down your recovery phrase from ChatT's settings first, then uninstall the old app before installing from the Play Store. You'll use the phrase to get your account back.

Why can we promise all that? ChatT doesn't run in a corporate data center. It runs on the Internet Computer, a decentralized public network — so there's no server bill paid for with your data, and no single company that can read, sell or switch off your conversations.
